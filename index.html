Why Steve Jobs Is The G.O.A.T

Steve Jobs the visionary behind Apple

Steve Jobs was a visionary entrepreneur and innovator who co-founded Apple Inc, founded NeXT, and funded Pixar. He is widely regarded as one of the greatest minds in the tech industry, known for his ability to anticipate consumer needs and create products that revolutionized the way we live and work.
